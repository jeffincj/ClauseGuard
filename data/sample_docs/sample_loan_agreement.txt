PERSONAL LOAN AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between QuickCash NBFC Ltd. ("Lender") and
Mr. Arjun Mehta ("Borrower") for a personal loan.

1. LOAN AMOUNT AND TENURE
The Lender agrees to disburse a loan of Rs. 3,00,000 to the Borrower for
a tenure of 36 months.

2. INTEREST RATE
The loan carries a floating interest rate, currently set at 14.5% per
annum. The Lender reserves the right to reset the rate at any time based
on internal benchmark rate changes, with 15 days notice to the Borrower.

3. PROCESSING FEES AND CHARGES
A one-time processing fee of 2.5% of the loan amount plus applicable taxes
shall be deducted from the disbursed amount. A mandatory loan protection
insurance premium of Rs. 8,500 shall also be deducted upfront.

4. PREPAYMENT
The Borrower may prepay the loan in part or full after 12 months from
disbursement. A foreclosure charge of 4% of the outstanding principal
shall apply to any prepayment made before the completion of 24 months.

5. LATE PAYMENT
In the event of delayed EMI payment, a penal interest of 3% per month
shall be charged on the overdue amount, compounded monthly until the
overdue amount is cleared in full.

6. COLLATERAL AND DEFAULT
This is an unsecured loan. In the event of default for 3 consecutive EMIs,
the Lender may report the default to credit bureaus and initiate recovery
proceedings, including engaging third-party recovery agents.

7. GUARANTOR
Mr. Sanjay Mehta acts as Guarantor for this loan. The Guarantor's liability
shall be co-extensive with that of the Borrower and shall extend to the
entire outstanding loan amount, including all penalties and charges, in
the event of Borrower default.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of India, with disputes subject to
the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Mumbai.

Signed,
Lender: QuickCash NBFC Ltd.
Borrower: Arjun Mehta
Guarantor: Sanjay Mehta
