RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is entered into between the Landlord, Mr. R. Subramaniam,
and the Tenant, Ms. Priya Nair, for the residential property located at
Flat 4B, Lakeview Apartments, Chennai.

1. TERM
The lease term shall be 11 months commencing from August 1, 2026, renewable
by mutual consent.

2. RENT
The monthly rent shall be Rs. 22,000, payable in advance on or before the
5th of every month. A late payment fee of Rs. 200 per day shall apply for
payments made after the 5th.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of Rs. 66,000 (three
months' rent) at the time of signing. The deposit shall be refunded within
30 days of vacating the premises, subject to deductions for damages beyond
normal wear and tear, unpaid utility bills, or unpaid rent dues.

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD
There shall be a lock-in period of 6 months from the commencement date.
Should the Tenant vacate before the completion of the lock-in period, the
Tenant shall forfeit the entire security deposit as a penalty, regardless
of the reason for vacating.

5. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall be responsible for routine maintenance including minor
plumbing and electrical repairs up to Rs. 1,000 per incident. The Landlord
shall be responsible for structural repairs and major appliance failures
not caused by tenant negligence.

6. RENT ESCALATION
Upon renewal after the initial 11-month term, the rent shall increase by
10% annually, at the Landlord's sole discretion, without requiring mutual
agreement.

7. NOTICE PERIOD
Either party may terminate this agreement after the lock-in period by
providing 60 days written notice. The Landlord may terminate with 30 days
notice if the Tenant is found in breach of any clause herein.

8. LANDLORD ENTRY
The Landlord reserves the right to enter the premises for inspection at
any time deemed necessary, with reasonable effort to inform the Tenant in
advance.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement shall be governed by the laws applicable in Tamil Nadu, India.

Signed,
Landlord: R. Subramaniam
Tenant: Priya Nair
Date: July 15, 2026
