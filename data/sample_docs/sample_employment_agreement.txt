EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT AND NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This agreement is entered into between NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
("Company") and Ms. Divya Krishnan ("Employee") for the role of Software
Engineer.

1. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee agrees to keep all proprietary information, source code,
client data, and business strategies confidential during employment and
for a period of 5 years after termination of employment, regardless of
the reason for termination.

2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT
All work product, inventions, and code created by the Employee during the
term of employment shall be the sole property of the Company. This
includes any work created using personal equipment or during personal
time, if related in any way to the Company's current or anticipated
business activities.

3. NON-COMPETE
Upon termination of employment for any reason, the Employee agrees not to
work for or consult with any competing company within the software
industry, anywhere in India, for a period of 12 months.

4. TRAINING BOND
The Company shall provide specialized onboarding training valued at
Rs. 1,50,000. Should the Employee resign within 18 months of joining, the
Employee shall be liable to reimburse the full training cost as
liquidated damages, payable within 15 days of resignation.

5. TERMINATION
The Company may terminate this agreement without cause by providing 30
days written notice or salary in lieu thereof. The Employee must provide
90 days written notice to resign.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be resolved through binding
arbitration in Bengaluru, in accordance with the Arbitration and
Conciliation Act. The Employee shall bear their own legal costs regardless
of outcome.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of India.

Signed,
Company: NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Employee: Divya Krishnan
Date: July 5, 2026
